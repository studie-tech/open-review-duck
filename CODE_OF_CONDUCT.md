# Code of conduct

ReviewDuck is committed to a welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific when giving feedback.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Assume good intent while remaining clear about technical and community
  boundaries.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, private source, personal data, or
  details from private vulnerability reports.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully and help keep discussions focused.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, personal attacks,
doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private
information are not tolerated. Repeatedly ignoring moderation or safety
guidance is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other project spaces. It also applies when someone publicly represents the
project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner using the contact
details on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue about a
conduct incident. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may warn, temporarily restrict,
or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this policy. Decisions
will consider the impact, context, and pattern of behavior, with the safety of
the community taking priority.
